# About HIVE 490

HIVE 490 is a beekeeping project based at Mt. Trashmore. This site hosts
**Beemail** — periodic updates on hive inspections, seasonal work, honey
harvests, and whatever else is happening in the apiary.

New Beemail posts go out no more than once a month. If you'd like them
delivered straight to your inbox, [subscribe here]({{ site.mailchimp_signup_url }}){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.
