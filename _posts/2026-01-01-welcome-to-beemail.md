Welcome to **Beemail**, the (very) occasional newsletter from HIVE 490 at
Mt. Trashmore. This is where I'll post updates on the hives — inspections,
splits, swarms, honey harvests, winter prep, and whatever else comes up
over the course of a season.

A few notes on what to expect:

- **Frequency:** roughly once a month, usually tied to something
  actually happening at the hives.
- **Format:** usually short updates.
- **Where it lives:** every update is posted here first, and also goes
  out by email to subscribers.

If you'd like future updates delivered to your inbox, use the Subscribe
link at the top of the page. Thanks for reading!
